Why this site exists

Smart manufacturing and AI are everywhere right now. Most of what gets written about them skips the hard part: the shopfloor systems, data and processes that decide how much value any of it delivers.

That's what I write about. Shopfloor AI and smart factory strategy, plus the MES and MOM foundations underneath them, from real implementations rather than brochures. Some of it is educational, for people early in the journey. Some is opinion, earned the hard way.

The goal is simple: elevate manufacturers to take advantage of available technologies and close the gap between AI capabilities and the practical reality of running a factory.

My story

Where it started

Computer science, then Lighthouse

I studied computer science at the University of Sheffield from 2007 to 2011, graduating with first class honours. During the degree I spent a year in industry at Lighthouse, a pure-play MES company, and went back after graduating. As a software engineer I built MES projects for customers and integrated our product, Shopfloor-Online, with everything around it.

Then

Projects and solution design

Managing implementations and designing solutions for manufacturers across industries and processes, all over the world.

2019

Head of Product

Leading the team responsible for the whole product: management, design, quality, release, documentation, pricing and go-to-market.

2021 to 2024

Director, then Senior Director of Product at Infor

Leading the product organisation for Infor MES from strategy through to market, and acting as the primary pre-sales lead. Along the way I completed MBA Essentials at the London School of Economics.

Since January 2024

VP & General Manager, MES at Infor

Globally responsible for the MES/MOM business and its P&L, from product strategy and go-to-market to customer success. Winner of Infor's Circle of Excellence in 2024.

Along the way I've been involved in countless MOM projects, in food and beverage, automotive, packaging, metal and plastics fabrication, industrial manufacturing and more. Most of what I write here comes from what I've learned through those projects.
