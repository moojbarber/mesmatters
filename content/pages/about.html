Why MES Matters exists

MES is the foundation of smart manufacturing and Industry 4.0. It's critical to the future of manufacturing, and yet it's still one of the most misunderstood pieces of the factory.

Most of what you'll read online is about buzzwords and the next big thing. This site goes the other way: the real-world problems manufacturers face, and the pragmatic answers that actually get them solved. Much of it is educational, written for people at the start of their MES journey. Some of it is opinion, earned the hard way.

The goal is simple: close the gap between futuristic ideas and the practical reality of running a factory.
