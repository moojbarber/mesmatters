MES MATTERS — LOGO PACK
=======================

WHICH FILE FOR WHAT
- LinkedIn profile banner ........ social/linkedin-personal-banner-1584x396.png
- LinkedIn company page cover .... social/linkedin-company-cover-1128x191.png
- LinkedIn company page logo ..... social/linkedin-company-logo-400x400.png
- X / Twitter header ............. social/x-twitter-header-1500x500.png
- Kit newsletter header .......... lockup-horizontal/mes-matters-horizontal-dark-bg-240px.png (on a dark email header)
                                   or ...-light-bg-240px.png (on a white email)
- Slides, documents (dark) ....... lockup-horizontal/...-dark-bg  or  lockup-stacked/...-dark-bg
- Slides, documents (white) ...... lockup-horizontal/...-light-bg or  lockup-stacked/...-light-bg
- Single colour on dark photo .... lockup-horizontal/mes-matters-horizontal-white
- App / profile icon, favicon .... icons/  (square for most uses, rounded where a platform doesn't round it for you)
- The mark on its own ............ mark/  (transparent background)

SVG files are vector: they scale to any size and the wordmark text is already
converted to outlines, so they look right without installing any fonts.
PNG files have transparent backgrounds (except icons and social images).

COLOURS
- Navy (background) ...... #0A0820
- Violet ................. #8B6CFF
- Cyan ................... #33D4E0
- Gradient ............... #8B6CFF -> #33D4E0, left to right / top-left to bottom-right
- Lilac (secondary) ...... #B9A9FF
- Body text on navy ...... #E9E6FF   Muted text ...... #A9A4D6

FONTS (free, Google Fonts)
- Headings and wordmark .. Space Grotesk Bold, uppercase, wide letter-spacing
- Body text .............. Inter

DO
- Use the dark-bg versions on navy or dark photos, light-bg versions on white.
- Leave clear space around the logo at least the width of one hexagon edge.

DON'T
- Stretch, rotate or recolour the mark.
- Put the dark-bg versions on white (the wordmark loses contrast).
- Place the logo on busy, mid-tone images.
